The sender needs Loom's existing pre-handler decision without host observation
authority. Explicitly accepting zen.DispatchRefused requests that fact through
ordinary later dispatch; the Switchboard remains the decision owner. A distinct
provenance kind authenticates it, and the notice creates no answer right.

Correlation alone cannot identify attempts: callers may reuse it or choose zero.
Reuse the existing queued sequence and carry it across the four addressed ABI
doors. This needs no global ledger, payload history or obligation tracker. ABI v7
refuses old binaries explicitly rather than silently changing their meaning.

Return to the author's original life and incarnation, checked at both queue
boundaries. Ordinary speech keeps its different life-only reload rule. Public
reasons coarsen the existing earlier sealed-target diagnostic under denied
authority, preserving concealment without rewriting host history.

TerminalSession consumes this ordinary mechanism. Its received-message entry
keeps the actual notice shape, while a structured authenticated detail names the
original send. This avoids forcing existing exhaustive TranscriptKind consumers
to add a top-level category, or mislabeling the original payload as newly
received. The Loom presentation reads the detail explicitly; later presentations
can adopt it through the same installed public API.

remain separate contracts. Current meaning belongs in the
